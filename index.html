UnicAthlete Scouting CRM

Purpose: A centralized workspace where scouts can discover, evaluate, track, contact, compare, and manage athletes through a structured recruiting pipeline.

Core Workflow: Discover → Save Prospect → Evaluate → Contact → Track Progress → Decision

My Profile | Dashboard | Discover Athletes | Prospects | Messages

1. Dashboard

The Dashboard is a visibility page. It shows what needs the scout's attention, including pipeline status, pending actions, recent activity, and athlete updates. No major work happens here; it helps the scout quickly understand what to review next.

UnicAthlete Scouting CRM

The Scouting CRM is a structured recruiting system that helps scouts discover, evaluate, contact, compare, and manage athletes through a centralized workflow.

Core Workflow

Discover → Save Prospect → Evaluate → Contact → Track Progress → Decision

System Components

Scout Profile

The Scout Profile serves as the recruiter's professional identity within UnicAthlete. It stores organizational information, verification details, scouting preferences, saved search criteria, activity summaries, and account settings while helping athletes and platform administrators verify the legitimacy of recruiting activity.

CRM Logic

Pipeline Statuses

New: Athlete saved to prospects.

Reviewing: Scout is evaluating athlete.

Contacted: Scout sent message or request.

Discussion: Athlete responded.

Decision: Signed, rejected, or archived.

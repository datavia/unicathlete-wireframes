Profile Completion & Recruiter-Ready

Athletes must complete a basic Player Profile during onboarding before reaching the dashboard. Recruiter-Ready is a separate marketplace status that unlocks recruiter discovery once the profile includes the minimum information needed for a recruiter to evaluate the athlete.

Player Profile

Required during onboarding. Includes identity, soccer profile, physical information, and basic player context.

Playing History

Playing History organizes an athlete's career into individual seasons. Each season stores club, competition, position, and match participation, allowing recruiters to evaluate the athlete's experience over time.

Season-Based Records

Every season is stored independently. Athletes can add new seasons while preserving previous playing history.

Career Timeline

Match Statistics

Each season tracks universal football statistics including matches played, starts, minutes played, disciplinary records, and the primary position for that season.

Universal Statistics

Season Progression

Athletes can continually add matches throughout the season, keeping statistics and playing history up to date as their career progresses.

Ongoing Updates

Documents & Information Requests

Athletes can upload documents proactively or provide them later in response to recruiter requests. Documents remain private by default and are only shared with recruiters when the athlete approves or submits them through an Information Request.

Academic

Education-related documents used primarily for university recruitment.

Standard Category

Medical

Medical clearance and health-related documents required by clubs or organizations.

Standard Category

Custom

Flexible documents defined by the athlete or requested by recruiters (for example: Coach Recommendation, Club Release, Parent Consent).

Flexible Category
Upload Document → Stored Privately → Recruiter Request → Athlete Shares Document
Privacy Logic

Documents are never publicly visible. Recruiters cannot browse an athlete's private documents. Documents become accessible only after the athlete shares them through the Information Request workflow.
